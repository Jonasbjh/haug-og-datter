---
title: 'Why Lun has no streaks'
date: 2026-06-24
app: lun
lang: en
---

Tenkt counts days in a row. It makes sense there: a morning puzzle is something you choose. Lun had streaks in the early sketches too, but they were removed.

A migraine diary is used on bad days. A counter that resets because you had a good week without opening the app punishes you for feeling well.

So Lun counts nothing you can lose. No streaks, no badges, no red dots. The app is the same no matter how long it has been.
