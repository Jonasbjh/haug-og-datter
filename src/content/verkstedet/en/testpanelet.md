Bumle Bjørn is tested by my daughter before every update. The feedback is easy to read: if something is boring, she puts the phone down. If something is good, she wants to play it again.

That is more precise than it sounds. Adult testers are polite and call things interesting. Kids are honest.

What survives here ships to other kids.
